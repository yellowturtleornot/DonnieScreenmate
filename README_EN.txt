====================================================
   DONNIE SCREENMATE — Version 1.0.0
====================================================

Hi! Donnie is your interactive assistant for the Windows desktop.

--- HOW TO LAUNCH ---
No installation required! Just double-click Donnie.exe and Donnie will start right away.
If you want Donnie to greet you every time you turn on your PC, add him to Startup (instructions below).

--- WHAT DONNIE CAN DO ---
1. Reacts to petting: move your mouse back and forth over his head to boost his mood.
2. Sits on windows: drag Donnie with your mouse and drop him over an open browser or folder window — he will land right on the top border.
3. Focus timer: click "Timer", enter your work duration (in min.), and Donnie will sit down at his laptop so he won't distract you.
4. Takes care of you: reminds you to drink water, stretch your back, and go to sleep on time (configurable in the "Reminders" menu).
5. Changes poses and phrases depending on his mood.

--- CONTROLS ---
• Left-click on Donnie — open / close the control menu.
• Click and hold the left mouse button — drag him anywhere on the screen.
• System tray icon (near the clock in the bottom-right corner) — hide or show Donnie, switch language (RU / EN), restart, or exit the app.

--- HOW TO ADD TO WINDOWS STARTUP ---
To make Donnie launch automatically when you turn on your PC:
1. Right-click on the Donnie.exe file and select "Create shortcut".
2. Press Win + R on your keyboard.
3. In the opened window, type: shell:startup and press Enter.
4. Drag the created shortcut into the opened folder. Done!

--- WHERE SAVES ARE STORED ---
Your progress and settings are saved automatically in your Windows user system folder. When a new version comes out, simply download and run it — all your progress will be preserved!
